Attire & Photo Etiquette

Attire Recommendation

  • Gentlemen: Formal Western attire (Dark suit / Formal shirt with tie & trousers with formal shoes).
  • Ladies: Elegant Saree / Formal western dress or formal trouser suit.
  • Coordinated, dignified color tones (Navy, Black, Maroon, Cream, or Royal hues recommended).

Important Instructions

  • Carry your Student Identity Card for smooth verification.
  • Kindly be seated on time to maintain the photographic lighting and schedule.
  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during official group shots.
